# Flight plan

**Thesis.** Hemant's career reads like a mission: a pre-launch period of ground tests (2022 to 2024 side projects), then a launch in June 2024 and six overlapping phases. Mission documents from the 1970s standards-manual era were plain, exact and legible. That matches an engineer who sells precision.

**Palette.** Paper (default): Ground #F4F5F3, Ink #121417, Ink soft #474C54, Signal red #D2291D. Orbit (dark): Ground #0D1219, Instrument white #E9EDF0, Signal red #FF5B41.

**Type.** Display: Chivo 800 (a 70s grotesk, not a Futura clone). Text: Public Sans (from the US federal design system, fits government documentation). Telemetry: Overpass Mono (Highway Gothic lineage).

**Layout.** A 12-column standards-manual page: an 8px red band, thick black rules above each section, figure numbers and captions for every drawing. Crew facts are set as a checklist with dotted leaders. A telemetry strip runs a live MET clock from T-0 (1 June 2024).

**Signature.** Fig. 2, the trajectory plot. Each role is a transfer arc spanning its real dates, with height equal to months served. Pre-launch sits in a hatched, compressed segment. The current phase is still climbing and continues as a dotted line. Pointer, touch or arrow keys scrub mission elapsed time. Active arcs, the phase list and the globe orbits turn red, and the readout names who was on board.

**3D.** One small orthographic globe: a hidden-line graticule, a launch-site marker at Mathura, and six orbits. Each orbit's inclination is months in phase × 4° and its node is the start month. Drag rotates it. It renders only on drag, scrub or theme change. One object is enough, because the plot carries the story.

**Motion.** A single 1.2s settle that turns Mathura to face the viewer, eased drag, and patch orbits rotating 14° on hover. Reduced motion gets a static pose.

**Pages.**
- Projects: missions, each with a generated patch (one orbit per technology). Maintained is nominal, in progress is in flight, archived is deorbited as a decay spiral.
- Experience: the full trajectory with per-phase briefing pages.
- Now: a dated status report log.
- Ask: capcom, a moderated channel with transcripts.
- About: the crew biography and checklist.
