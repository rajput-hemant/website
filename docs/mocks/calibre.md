# Calibre

**Thesis.** "Pixel-perfect" and "fast" are the same promise watchmakers make with tolerances and rate. The portfolio becomes one movement, Calibre HR-26, and every figure on it is true. Jewels: 14, one per project. Frequency: 3 Hz (21,600 vph), one hertz per stack (web, server, mobile). Complications: 5, the core skills. Power reserve: fully wound, meaning available for work. The service record is a 36-month subdial with six role arcs.

**Palette.**
- Salmon dial `#E9C2AE` (light ground)
- Dial ink `#221C1A`
- Blued steel `#1C3491` (hands, links, focus)
- Ruby `#A3203F` (jewels only)
- Rose gold `#D2A07F` (chatons; the dark theme's warm accent)
- Rhodium night `#15171A` (dark theme, the caseback side)

**Type.** Display: Bodoni Moda, the dial printer's Didone, used for the name, numerals and Roman hour marks. Text: Alegreya Sans. Utility: Alegreya Sans SC with lining, tabular figures, set like a technical sheet.

**Layout.** At 1440 the page reads like a catalogue spread: identity and power reserve on the left, the caseback in the centre, the technical sheet and service record on the right. Projects are jewel cards. Each has a 14-position map with its own jewel set in ruby.

**Signature.** The beat. The balance swings at 3 Hz, and a blued rim index moves one step per beat. At rest it runs as a seconds hand, 1 degree per beat. When you hover a nav item it jumps an hour per beat to that section's mark (XII Projects, III Experience, VI Lab, IX About).

**3D.** A procedural movement seen through a sapphire caseback. It has extruded gears with spoked crossings, Geneva-striped bridges, a perlage plate, a balance with a hairspring, a pallet fork, blued screws and exactly 14 jewels: 12 in chatons plus 2 pallet stones. It uses one canvas with DPR capped at 1.5 and renders 12 frames a second only while visible. It stops under reduced motion and shows an SVG stand-in if WebGL is missing. I use 3D only in the hero, because the watch should be the one precious object on the page.

**Motion.** Stepped, never eased loops: discrete ticks, and damped drag only.

**Pages.** Projects: the full 14-jewel register. Experience: the service-record subdial, enlarged. Now: a rate log of dated regulation entries. Ask: an engraving request book, moderated. About: the watchmaker's bench notes.
