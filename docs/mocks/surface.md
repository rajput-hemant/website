# Control surface

**Thesis.** Hemant builds fast, exact interfaces. A precision instrument is the honest metaphor: every control does one thing, every number is real. The site is the HR-26 (his initials, 2026 revision), and the page is its faceplate.

**Palette.**
- Powder coat `#D5D2CA` (grey edition plate)
- Black anodise `#151514` (black edition plate)
- Legend ink `#1A1A18`
- LCD green-grey `#AAB397`
- Signal yellow `#F2B705`: the colour of the single result key on a Rams-era calculator. It marks only what is live: the selected channel, the knob's index, "available". Never text.

**Type.** Barlow Semi Condensed for legends and display (DIN-lineage engraving). Barlow for text. Doto for the one dot-matrix line. Numerals are hand-built 7-segment SVG.

**Layout.** A 12-column faceplate of modules split by milled seams: identity, selector, readout (CH, since 2022, 14 projects, 6 roles), status, and an aluminium rating plate listing the stack.

**Signature.** The channel selector. Five detents are the five routes (00 Home to 04 About). Hovering a detent or a header key turns the knob and changes the LCD's CH digit. You can drag the knob, or press 0 to 4.

**3D.** 3D-light on purpose. The knob is the only 3D object: a lathe-turned three.js body with turning marks in its roughness, 150 knurl ribs, and an inlaid yellow index, rendered only when something changes. Drag it through sprung detents; it leans toward a mouse and sinks when pressed. Everything else stays printed and flat, because a real faceplate is. Without WebGL the SVG knob remains. On inner pages it docks into the header and turns on route change.

**Motion.** Sprung detents with slight overshoot, 150ms LED fades, a pulse only for in-progress work. The motion switch stops all of it.

**Pages.**
- Projects: preset banks (4 per bank, 14 presets); LEDs show status.
- Experience: a multitrack tape view of six roles overlapping from 2024 to 2026.
- Now/changelog: the dot-matrix log.
- Ask: an input module with a queue readout of pending and answered questions.
- About: the rating plate, expanded into an operating manual.
