# Maquette

**Thesis.** A study model proves a plan can be built, and is honest about what is finished and what is still frame. Hemant's work becomes a site model on a basswood plinth.

**Palette.** Model card `#EFEFEB`, room `#E3E4E1`, graphite `#202326`, archive grey `#A7AAA7`, basswood `#D2B386` (text and focus in cut edge `#76552A`). At night the room goes to `#101113` under one spotlight.

**Type.** Jost for display and engraved plaque caps, in the Futura lineage of model-shop lettering. Albert Sans for text. Chivo Mono for readouts and dates.

**Layout.** Name left, model right with plaques at the plinth edge, phasing plan and shadow study below. Project cards are vitrines with an elevation and a plan.

**Rule.** Height is one storey per year since the first commit. Footprint is one bay per technology in the stack, taken from `content/fallback/projects.ts`: Infinitunes 8, JioSaavn API 3, Lipi 8, leetcode 5. Material shows state: white card is maintained, a basswood frame with an open top storey is in progress, grey card is archived. The other 10 projects are foam context blocks. Experience is six dated phases, and the current one is basswood.

**Signature.** A real shadow study. The pointer sets the time of day, and the sun position comes from solar geometry for Mathura (27.49° N) on 26 September 2026. The model's shadows, the sun-path arc and every plan shadow in the cards sweep together. At night one spotlight swings instead.

**3D.** One canvas holds the plinth, card slabs with recessed reveals (so you can count storeys), dowel trees and one scale figure. Lighting is a hemisphere light plus a directional light with soft PCF shadows and a contact-shadow plane. It renders on demand, turns on drag, holds still under reduced motion, and falls back to SVG. One 3D object is enough: the model is the idea.

**Motion.** Shadows ease across the model. Hovering a plaque lifts its piece off the site.

**Pages.** Projects: all 14 pieces in vitrines. Experience: the full phasing plan. Now: model revisions. Ask: comment cards pinned to the model. About: a studio wall label.
